DMCA & Copyright Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

APK STORE respects the intellectual property rights of developers and publishers. We publish apps from our own studio and selected legitimate open-source projects under their respective licences. If you believe a listing infringes your copyright, we will act promptly on a valid request.

Filing a takedown request

Send us a notice through the channels on our Contact page with the subject line “DMCA Takedown” and include:

  • Your full name and the company or developer you represent.
  • The name of the app or content and the exact URL of the listing on this site.
  • Proof that you own the copyright or are authorised to act for the owner (for example, a link to your official app listing or project repository).
  • A statement that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate.

What happens next

  • We review valid notices within 2 working days and remove or disable access to the disputed listing while we verify the claim.
  • The original uploader or developer is notified and may file a counter-notification if they believe the removal was a mistake.
  • Repeat infringers are permanently removed from the catalogue.

Counter-notification

If your listing was removed and you believe you hold the rights to it, contact us with your details, the removed listing URL, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of Pakistan and will accept service of process from the original complainant.
