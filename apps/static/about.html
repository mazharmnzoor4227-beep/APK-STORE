About APK STORE

Independent · Verified · Free

APK STORE is an independent Android app store built for people who want a clean, fast and trustworthy way to discover and download Android apps — without the clutter.

What we do

  • Signature-verified downloads — every APK published here is checked against its developer signing certificate before it goes live, and the verification status is shown on each app page.
  • Honest listings — real version numbers, real file sizes, screenshots and changelogs. No fake download buttons, no bundled installers.
  • Update alerts — the APK STORE Android app (package com.apkstore.client) notifies you when new versions of your apps are available.
  • Privacy first — browsing and downloading work without an account. Optional sign-in only syncs your favorites.

What we publish

We publish apps from our own studio and selected legitimate open-source projects, always respecting each project's licence. All trademarks and app names belong to their respective developers.

Get the app

The APK STORE Android app gives you faster downloads, automatic update alerts and offline access to your download history. You can install it from the banner or install dialog on our home page.
