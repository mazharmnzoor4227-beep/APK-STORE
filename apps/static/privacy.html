Privacy Policy

Updated 27 September 2026

About the service

APK STORE displays approved Android apps. The public website and Android app fetch catalog entries from Supabase. Downloads use Supabase Storage; the APK STORE Android installer is also available directly on this website.

Data used

Searching the website sends your search term to the catalog service. The site stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser. The Android app filters search terms on your device and stores its theme preference locally. To show updates, the Android app reads installed package IDs and version codes on your device; it does not send the installed app inventory to the catalog service. Public browsing does not require an account. We do not include advertising or analytics SDKs in the Android app.

Service logs

Our hosting providers may process technical request information such as IP address, requested URL and time in service logs. Downloads are opened in your browser. Supabase describes its own practices in its privacy notice.

Your choices

Clear your browser site data or Android app data to remove the stored theme choice. Delete downloaded APK files in your browser or device file manager. Questions can be raised through the APK STORE repository.
