Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

APK STORE lets you discover and download Android apps. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Downloads you request — when you tap download, we serve the APK file. We log anonymous download counts to show popularity.
  • Crash reports — if you use the APK STORE Android app and it crashes, an anonymous report (device model, OS version, stack trace) may be sent so we can fix bugs. No personal data is included.
  • Preferences on your device — theme choice (dark/light) and dismissed banners are stored in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses.
  • No sale of data.
  • We don't scan your installed apps or personal files.

Advertising

APK STORE shows ads served by Google AdSense to keep the service free. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and you can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings — the site keeps working without them. Learn more in Google's advertising policies.

APK safety

Every APK published on APK STORE is signature-verified before release. We show the verification status on each app page. Always verify unusual permission requests yourself before installing any app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us through the APK STORE Android app's About page.
